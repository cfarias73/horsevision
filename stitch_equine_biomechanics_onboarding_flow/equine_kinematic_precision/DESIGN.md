## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a dialogue between bespoke equestrian heritage and high-fidelity algorithmic biomechanics. It serves elite sport-horse trainers, equine orthopedic veterinarians, and discerning stud managers who require uncompromising computational rigor delivered through a medium of quiet prestige.

The emotional signature balances regal composure with clinical exactitude:
- **Heritage & Prestige:** Unhurried, structured space anchored by deep British Racing Green, conveying centuries of equestrian tradition and physical pedigree.
- **Biomechanical Precision:** Micro-telemetry badges, coordinate readouts, and surgical diagnostic metrics that inspire immediate institutional confidence.
- **Kinematic Dynamism:** Laser-sharp electric cyan and telemetry magenta accents indicating joint tracking points, vector paths, and stride analysis without veering into consumer-fitness gimmickry.

The design philosophy combines **Tactile Modern Luxury** with **High-Precision Scientific Instrument HUDs**. Large porcelain surfaces and pure white floating substrates are held by hairline boundaries, accented with warm muted brass-gold iconography, and punctuated by ultra-refined monospaced kinematic telemetry chips.

## Colors

The system uses a calibrated dual-nature palette: an organic, grounded base for clinical comfort paired with spectral telemetry accents for computer vision tracking.

### Primary & Core Surfaces
- **Primary Sovereign Green (`#0D3F2E`)**: Anchors primary actions, navigation headers, and hero confirmation points. A deeper variant (`#08291E`) provides ultra-high contrast backdrops for gait telemetry viewports.
- **Porcelain Canvas (`#F8FAF8`)**: Default background tone, avoiding sterile clinical glare through an imperceptible warm sage undertone.
- **Sage Muted Substrate (`#F0F4F1`)**: For recessed wells, segmented track bars, and unselected structural items.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Strictly for elevated card containers, interactive modals, and floating focal surfaces.

### Luxury & Verification
- **Equestrian Gold (`#C59B27`)**: Denotes premium diagnostic tiers, accreditation certifications, calibration verification checks, and milestone achievements. A lighter tint (`#F3E5AB`) serves as glowing fill for selected luxury badges.

### Computer Vision Telemetry (Functional Overlays)
- **Kinematic Cyan (`#00E5FF` / `#00B4D8`)**: Reserved strictly for AI skeleton tracking nodes (fetlock, hock, stifle, poll), joint angle vectors, and real-time inference indicators.
- **Diagnostic Magenta (`#FF2E93` / `#E02475`)**: Denotes asymmetry warnings, overload alerts, limb phase divergence, and high-priority onboarding prompts.

### Neutrals & Hierarchy
- **Hunter Charcoal (`#11221B`)**: High-contrast, optical black alternative for all primary headlines, key data figures, and structural lines.
- **Slate Secondary (`#556960`)**: Subtitles, unit descriptors, inactive state copy, and baseline telemetry grids.
- **Porcelain Border Line (`#E2E8E2`)**: Razor-thin structural dividers and card envelopes.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional contrast between human editorial warmth and cold laboratory calculation.

- **Primary Typeface (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its sculpted modern geometric grotesque character, refined proportions, and humanist terminals. It delivers commanding executive titles without aggressive corporate hardness.
- **Telemetry & Instrument Typeface (JetBrains Mono):** Dedicated to computational precision. All kinematics coordinates, frame indices, degree angles, velocity calculations, and sensor status tags use monospaced figures to ensure tabular stability during dynamic onboarding previews.

### Composition Guidelines
- Headlines use negative letter-spacing for athletic density.
- Telemetry tags must render strictly in tabular numerals (`tnum`), paired with micro-uppercase labels.
- Onboarding question headers never exceed three lines on mobile devices to preserve visual priority for interactive inputs.

## Layout & Spacing

The onboarding architecture operates on an adaptive vertical rhythm optimized for one-handed mobile grip:

### Mobile Layout Model
- **Canvas Base:** 4-column layout with fixed outer margin (`1.25rem` / `20px`) and `0.75rem` (`12px`) inner gutters.
- **Top Safe Zone (Navigation / Progress):** Dedicated `64px` tall zone accommodating step index dots, calibration badges, and discreet back controls.
- **Focus Stage (Dynamic Viewport):** Flexible card canvas occupying 55% to 65% of screen height for camera angle framing guides, horse model selection, or biomechanical metric visualizers.
- **Bottom Action Drawer:** Fixed bottom docking anchor (minimum padding `1.25rem`, bottom safe-area inset aware) with elevated primary action buttons and ancillary links.

### Spacing Principles
- Element-to-label separation strictly follows micro spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`).
- Functional groupings within onboarding option cards use `space-md`.
- Inter-card structural spacing uses `space-lg` to prevent inadvertent touch-target collisions.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth mimics high-end tactile instrument panels through translucent porcelain tiers, hairline borders, and atmospheric ambient diffusion.

### Hierarchy Levels
1. **Base Ground (Level 0):** Porcelain background (`#F8FAF8`). Completely non-elevated.
2. **Structural Interactive Surface (Level 1):** Pure white cards (`#FFFFFF`) sitting on `#F8FAF8`. Outlined with a razor-thin border (`1px solid #E2E8E2`). Supported by a diffused ambient shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(17, 34, 27, 0.04)`.
3. **Floating Controls & Modals (Level 2):** Elevated interactive pickers and contextual tooltip balloons. Supported by deep organic green-tinted shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(13, 63, 46, 0.08)`.
4. **Cinematic Telemetry Glass (Level 3):** Used in camera viewports and interactive sensor calibration screens. Frosted glass treatment with `rgba(8, 41, 30, 0.75)` backdrop fill, `16px` backdrop-filter blur, and an inner inset stroke of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
5. **Active Glow Signifiers:** Active AI tracking anchors and primary green CTA focus rings emit a micro-glow (`0px 0px 16px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.40)` for AI, and `0px 8px 24px rgba(13, 63, 46, 0.25)` for CTAs).

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances organic anatomic curvature with precision instrument enclosures.

- **Primary Components (Cards, Sheets, Interactive Selection Tiles):** Bound by `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`), generating a soft, sculpted profile that softens medical clinicalism into luxury ergonomics.
- **Buttons & Core CTAs:** Standardized at `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) for tactile firmness under touch.
- **Telemetry Chips & Micro Badges:** Encapsulated in pill profiles (`rounded-full`) to delineate them from interactive touch targets.
- **Indicator Nodes:** Strictly circular geometries (1:1 ratio) for camera crosshairs, joint markers, and step pagination nodes.

## Components

### 1. Primary Action Buttons
- **Default State:** British Racing Green (`#0D3F2E`) background, text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) using `label-lg`, height `56px`, corner radius `16px`. Subtle directional ambient drop: `0px 8px 20px rgba(13, 63, 46, 0.22)`.
- **Accent Luxury Variant:** Deep forest green background accented with a `1.5px` perimeter stroke in Muted Warm Gold (`#C59B27`) with gold typographic title.
- **Pressed State:** Micro-scale transformation (`scale(0.98)`), background darkening to `#08291E`.

### 2. Selection & Onboarding Option Cards
- White container (`#FFFFFF`), `1px` border in `#E2E8E2`, `20px` internal padding, `20px` corner radius.
- **Selected State:** Border shifts dynamically to British Racing Green (`2px solid #0D3F2E`) with a soft internal tint (`rgba(13, 63, 46, 0.03)`).
- A gold accent badge (`#C59B27`) appears in the top-right corner containing an animated precision checkmark.

### 3. Step & Gait Progress Indicators
- Centered horizontal layout: `8px` diameter inactive pagination circles in `#E2E8E2`.
- Active step transforms into a dynamic pill (`24px` width, `8px` height) rendered in British Racing Green (`#0D3F2E`) with an electric cyan telemetry dot nestled at the lead edge.

### 4. Precision Telemetry Chips & Badges
- **Status HUD Badges:** JetBrains Mono (`telemetry-data`), `28px` height, `4px 10px` padding.
- Semi-translucent dark slate background (`rgba(17, 34, 27, 0.06)`) with `1px` border in `#E2E8E2`.
- Active AI node badges utilize Kinematic Cyan text (`#00B4D8`) with an embedded pulsating neon cyan tracking light (`#00E5FF`).

### 5. Input Fields & Parameter Steppers
- Height `52px`, background `#F8FAF8`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8E2`, corner radius `12px`.
- Left-aligned label in Hunter Charcoal (`#11221B`), right-aligned unit indicator (e.g., `hh`, `kg`, `cm`) in JetBrains Mono (`telemetry-unit`, `#556960`).
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to British Racing Green (`#0D3F2E`) with a delicate secondary outer ring in `rgba(13, 63, 46, 0.12)`.

### 6. Kinematic Calibration Viewfinder (Onboarding Camera Assistant)
- Translucent dark overlay card (`#08291E` at 85% opacity) with high-blur backdrop filter (`20px`).
- Viewfinder corners indicated by `2px` hairline brackets in Muted Gold (`#D4AF37`).
- Target anatomic points (Poll, Wither, Point of Hip, Hock) marked with `12px` dual-ring nodes in Neon Cyan (`#00E5FF`), displaying instantaneous coordinate readings in JetBrains Mono.
