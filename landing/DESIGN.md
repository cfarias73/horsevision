## Brand & Style
This design system bridges high-end Swiss horological engineering with the biomechanical kinetics of elite equestrian motion capture. The aesthetic reflects absolute discipline, gallery-scale stillness, and surgical precision. 

Rather than leaning into traditional equestrian tropes of muddy earth tones or heavy racing greens, the visual identity draws from architectural exhibition spaces, skeletonized watch calibers, and clinical telemetry interfaces. Whitespace functions as structural mass rather than emptiness; every element exists with intentional placement, hairline tolerances, and uncompromising typographic hierarchy. The emotional response is one of silent authority, scientific lucidity, and bespoke luxury.

## Colors
The palette operates with gallery restraint, structured across high-tonal purity and surgical contrast:

- **Primary (`#0B0D0C`)**: Deep obsidian black. Anchors the primary typographic scale, strict geometric dividers, and high-impact editorial blocks. Never softened or diluted for body copy.
- **Secondary (`#C8A265`)**: Horological champagne gold. Reserved strictly for curated luxury markers: state indicators, biometric grade certifications, pedigree timestamps, and subtle telemetry calibrations.
- **Tertiary (`#00E5FF`)**: Surgical kinetic cyan. Used with surgical scarcity for active biomechanical vectors, sensor lock states, trajectory overlays, and live data telemetry points.
- **Neutral (`#9E9E98`)**: Precision mid-gray for secondary metadata, inactive tracking nodes, and hairline frame boundaries.
- **Base Canvas & Container Layers**: The foundation is built upon gallery warm white (`#FAFAF8`), layering into titanium ivory (`#F4F4F1`) for analytical card beds, with hairline borders defined in surgical neutral gray (`#E8E8E4`).

## Typography
The typographical voice combines the high-contrast elegance of an archival editorial publication with the uncompromising clarity of chronometric dials and laboratory telemetry.

- **Display & Headlines (`Newsreader`)**: Set with optical discretion, utilizing fluid baseline cadence and generous vertical leading. Headlines should evoke collector monographs and fine watchmaking indices. Italic variations are reserved strictly for scientific definitions, gait categorizations (e.g., *piaffe*, *passage*), or pedigree attributions.
- **Interface & Narrative Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutral, contemporary Swiss geometric sans-serif ensuring immediate scanning across dense kinetic metric dashboards and equine physical diagnostic summaries.
- **Scientific Telemetry & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict, monospaced tabular data for vector coordinates, angle metrics, Hertz rates, and sensor microsecond timestamps. Rendered in all-caps with generous tracking for maximum mechanical legibility.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined 12-column architectural grid on desktop (8-column on tablet, 4-column on mobile) with generous structural framing. Space is deliberately over-allocated around analytical telemetry to prevent cognitive overload.

- **Margins & Rhythm**: Desktop viewports anchor with an expansive 48px (`3rem`) canvas margin, ensuring visualizations never touch viewport boundaries. Gutters maintain a rigid 24px (`1.5rem`) corridor.
- **Grid Strictness**: Data points, horological indicators, and typography strictly snap to vertical axes. Empty grid zones are embraced as negative volume, reflecting gallery display vitrines.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints**:
  - *Desktop (≥1280px)*: Full 12-column split, allowing asymmetric 8:4 splits between real-time 3D skeletal capture and analytical breakdown dossiers.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1279px)*: 8-column layout with 24px margins; analytical cards stack beneath telemetry viewports.
  - *Mobile (<768px)*: 4-column layout with 20px margins; telemetry readouts convert from wide tabular matrices into indexed horizontal step-cards.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is expressed through tonal layering and structural hairline separation rather than heavy physical drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layers**: The background canvas rests on pure gallery warm white (`#FAFAF8`). Elevated analytical zones, charts, and control decks transition to titanium ivory (`#F4F4F1`). Floating interactive overlays and real-time kinetic feeds utilize high-contrast solid obsidian (`#0B0D0C`) with white and cyan text.
- **Hairline Framing**: Structural separation is governed by 1px borders colored `#E8E8E4`. There are zero thick borders; lines mimic the precise milling of a watch case.
- **Whisper Shadows**: When floating modules (e.g., inspector flyouts, lens selectors) detach from the canvas, depth is provided by an ultra-subtle, ambient diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(11, 13, 12, 0.04), 0 0 0 1px #E8E8E4`.
- **Telemetry Glow**: Kinetic tracking markers in surgical cyan (`#00E5FF`) can utilize a 4px soft micro-blur (`rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.25)`) to communicate active infrared sensor data capture against dark canvas overlays.

## Shapes
Geometry is engineered, crisp, and disciplined. Soft rounding (`0.25rem` / `4px`) is utilized strictly to take the harsh digital edge off precision modules while retaining structural architectural formality.

- Standard interactive components (buttons, input fields, and metric tiles) adhere to `roundedness: 1` (`4px`).
- Sensor dot markers and status indicators remain pure circles (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- Large container surfaces and visual media viewports maintain either `4px` or `8px` (`rounded-lg`) maximum radius, ensuring an engineered, sapphire-crystal watch aesthetic rather than a consumer app feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic**: Obsidian background (`#0B0D0C`), white text (`#FAFAF8`), `4px` radius, styled with `JetBrains Mono` label-md typography. Hover introduces a hairline inset border in secondary gold (`#C8A265`).
- **Telemetry Action**: Outlined in 1px `#E8E8E4`, transparent background, obsidian text. Hover shifts background to titanium ivory (`#F4F4F1`) and border to `#0B0D0C`.
- **Kinetic Override**: Obsidian background with surgical cyan (`#00E5FF`) active text and a continuous micro-pulse indicator for live telemetry recording.

### Metric Cards & Telemetry Vitrines
- Constructed on a titanium ivory surface (`#F4F4F1`) with 1px hairline border in `#E8E8E4`.
- Header houses uppercase monospaced labels (`label-sm`) in `#9E9E98`, with values rendered in `Newsreader` (`headline-sm`) for horological gravity.
- Minor biometric deviations are annotated with inline tags tinted with secondary champagne (`#C8A265`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat surfaces framed with a 1px border (`#E8E8E4`).
- Focused state transitions the border to `#0B0D0C` with zero fuzzy focus rings, maintaining absolute edge sharpness.
- Placeholder copy rests at `#9E9E98` in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Checkboxes are 16x16px squares with a 1px border. Checked state fills obsidian (`#0B0D0C`) with a crisp champagne (`#C8A265`) verification mark.
- Segmented switches use an ivory frame containing a slide-indicator with a 1px border, echoing the bezel of a split-seconds chronograph.

### Biomechanical Trace Nodes & Overlays
- Live vector overlays incorporate fine hairline grids (`0.5px` dashed `#E8E8E4`).
- Joint coordinates (hocks, fetlocks, poll, stifle) display as 6px `#0B0D0C` discs bounded by 12px translucent surgical cyan halos (`#00E5FF`), displaying instantaneous degree of flexion in tracking labels.
